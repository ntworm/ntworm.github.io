## This Feminine Side

An animated documentary directed by Alice Siniscalchi and produced through <a class="entity-link" href="https://www.reanima.eu/films/this-feminine-side" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">RE:Anima</a> and Lusófona Films. Set in Lisbon’s LGBTQ+ artistic scene, the film layers interviews, collaborative workshops, textures, and colour to explore femininity beyond the binary as something fluid and collectively reinvented.

I came on as Sound Director and handled the sound design and editing, with assistance from Miguel Colimão. The work had to hold together recorded voices, intimate gestures, and playful mixed-media animation without flattening the differences between them.

The 7:28 film was selected for the <a class="entity-link" href="https://itfs.de/film/this-feminine-side/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ITFS 2026 Student Competition</a> in Stuttgart and for Zinegoak’s 2026 touring programme. It is one of the works that most clearly anchors my move from cinema-only sound into a broader audiovisual practice.
