## This Feminine Side

An audiovisual piece produced within the <a class="entity-link" href="https://www.re-anima.pt" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">REANIMA</a> program at <a class="entity-link" href="https://www.ulusofona.pt" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Universidade Lusófona</a>, in the same context as <a class="entity-link" href="/work/el-tono-del-mar">El Tono Del Mar</a>. This is [Alice](https://www.instagram.com/twistedpasta/)'s final project — a master's thesis film she directed in Lisbon.

I came on as Sound Director and did all the sound design and editing, with assistance from Miguel Colimão. We worked together with Alice inside the Lusófona context.

The piece is the one I have the most media to publish about — it ran longer and wider than expected, and it is the work that anchors my move from cinema-only sound into the broader audiovisual space.
