## Um Certo João Silvino

A feature film written and directed by <a class="entity-link" href="https://www.instagram.com/heliobritofilmes/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Hélio Brito</a> that mixes documentary and fiction. It freely reconstructs a real episode from the turn of 1971 to 1972: a young stranger who calls himself João Silvino arrives in the quiet town of Natividade, in the Tocantins interior, during the military dictatorship. He was Ruy Carlos Vieira Berbert, a University of São Paulo student and political militant, who was arrested in the town and found dead in his cell days later. Buried as an unidentified man, his body has never been found.

The fiction was shot in Natividade in May 2025, with Felipe Trindade as João Silvino, and the documentary part brings in archive footage and scenes shot in Goiânia, São Paulo, and Palmas. The film was made through the Lei Paulo Gustavo in Tocantins and premiered on 13 November 2026 at the <a class="entity-link" href="https://www.festivalcinetoca.com.br/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">3rd CINETOCA</a>, the Grand Prize of Tocantins Cinema, in its competitive feature-film showcase, screening at Cine Cultura in Palmas.

## Sound finishing

I took over the film's sound in the last stretch before the festival premiere, working in Pro Tools from the editor's AAF against the 89-minute picture, with the complete production sound of the shoot (more than 50 GB across three weeks of shooting days) at hand to replace or repair what the edit carried.

The work was corrective and focused. I smoothed the abrupt audio cuts left in the edit, so scenes stop jumping at every splice, and balanced score against dialogue and narration across the whole film, so the music no longer covers a line and a line no longer drops into a hole. I also synced and treated João Silvino's voice-over narration, recorded separately and delivered with the script's timings, placing each passage on its cue and matching it to the sound of the film around it.
