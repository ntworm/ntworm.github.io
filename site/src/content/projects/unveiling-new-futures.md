## Unveiling New Futures

Two ads in Universidade Lusófona's 2025 "Descobrir Novos Futuros" / "Unveiling New Futures" international campaign — Lusófona's main international push of the year, running across Portugal and other markets. Sound Director and Composer on both: captured all the production sound, composed the original music, mixed and delivered.

The campaign ran as two separate deliverables with their own sound design and original score, sitting inside the same visual and musical language. Full ownership of the sound track, from set capture through final delivery.
