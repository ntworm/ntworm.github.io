## fxhash — generative work as nt_worm

Around thirty pieces on [fxhash archive](https://killedbyapixel.github.io/fxhashArchive/#/artist/tz1du7JyqkpAWrQf7kspXS383wpMxkVWCoqQ), all using <a class="entity-link" href="https://hydra.ojack.xyz" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Hydra.js</a> for visuals and Web Audio API + OSC for sound reactivity. The body of work sits between two poles: ambient loops driven by audio analysis, and glitch-driven compositions that fork from the same Hydra patches but treat the canvas as a per-frame decision space.

KAKOFONI ORQUESTRA (edition #37 acquired by the Madison Museum) and the <a class="entity-link" href="/work/lucy">LUCY</a> tracks are the pieces with their own case pages on this site. The full archive lives at the profile link above.

This is also the loose research lab for the larger pieces — most of what I learn about Hydra + Web Audio here ends up feeding back into <a class="entity-link" href="/work/lucy">LUCY</a> or into a live show.
