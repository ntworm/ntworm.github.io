## Performance with Adriana Sá

A live audiovisual performance at Universidade Lusófona, in Lisbon, in 2023, made with <a class="entity-link" href="https://adrianasa.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Adriana Sá</a>, my teacher in the Sonic Arts course (Artes Sónicas). For it, the group built an instrument, the noise machine: a wooden box with strings, metal rulers and a spring.

## Signal chain

- **Instrument:** the noise machine, built by the group.
- **Processing:** sound, image and video processed in real time with <a class="entity-link" href="https://hydra.ojack.xyz" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Hydra</a>, a small modular system and a Boss VT-1.
- **Output:** projection in the room.

The photos show the noise machine, laptops running Hydra and the modular system with the VT-1. The noise machine went on to be part of the <a class="entity-link" href="/work/welcome-week-installation">welcome week installation</a> at the same university.
