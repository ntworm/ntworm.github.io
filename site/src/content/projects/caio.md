## Caio

A 2024 BA short film from Universidade Lusófona. Remix Engineer on the piece — took the existing soundtrack and shaped the final re-mix for delivery.

The credit here was post-production re-mixing rather than original composition. The work is making sure every layer of the original mix reads cleanly under the picture and lands at the right level for each scene — closer to editorial sound work than to original sound design or composition.
