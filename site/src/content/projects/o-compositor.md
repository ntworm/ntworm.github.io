## O Compositor

An independent BA short film produced at <a class="entity-link" href="https://www.ulusofona.pt" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Universidade Lusófona</a>. As Sound Director, I owned every sound-related decision on the project, from the initial concept to the final mix: on-set recording, composition and recording of orchestral musicians, sound editing, and sound design.

The film's premise — a composer who can't hear his own work — demanded a soundscape that lived between performance and abstraction. The orchestral recording was the spine: real instruments captured with intimate close-miking, then folded into dialogue and atmospheric beds through a long session of editing and design. The result was strong enough to land a nomination at <a class="entity-link" href="https://www.imdb.com/pt/event/ev0074294/2026/1/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prémio Curtas 2026</a> in the Best Composition (Melhor Composição) category — a Portuguese short film award. The nomination was for the original music composed for the film.
