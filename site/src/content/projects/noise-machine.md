## Noise Machine

A project made by a group of students at Universidade Lusófona, in Lisbon, in 2023, supervised by <a class="entity-link" href="https://adrianasa.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Adriana Sá</a>. We built an instrument, the noise machine, a wooden box with strings, metal rulers and a spring, and presented it in a room at the university.

## Signal chain

- **Instrument:** the noise machine, built by the group.
- **Processing:** sound, image and video processed in real time with <a class="entity-link" href="https://hydra.ojack.xyz" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Hydra</a>, a small modular system and a Boss VT-1.
- **Output:** projection in the room.

The photos show the noise machine, laptops running Hydra and the modular system with the VT-1. The noise machine later became part of an <a class="entity-link" href="/work/lusofona-av-performance">audiovisual performance open to the public</a> at the same university.
