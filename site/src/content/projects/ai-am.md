## A.I. AM

An independently produced 2024 science-fiction short directed by Danny J. In a post-apocalyptic world dominated by rogue artificial intelligence, Alpha-30 and Mew — one of the last human survivors — must convince an obsolete AI sentry of their identity to enter a Safe Zone.

I composed the film’s original score, building the musical perspective around the tension between human vulnerability and automated control.
