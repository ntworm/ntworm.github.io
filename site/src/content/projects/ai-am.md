## A.I. AM

A short sci-fi film out of <a class="entity-link" href="https://www.ulusofona.pt" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Universidade Lusófona</a>. I was the composer for the soundtrack: nearly everything you hear is a single steel-string acoustic guitar, recorded at the university studios in Lisbon.

Working alone on a steel guitar for a sci-fi short is a constraint I chose on purpose. It strips the score down to gesture — each note has to carry the scene on its own. Most of the choices were about *what to leave out*: no synth pads, no reverb tails longer than a breath, no orchestral doubling. Just the guitar, close-mic'd, against picture.
