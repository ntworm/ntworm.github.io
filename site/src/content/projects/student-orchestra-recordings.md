## Student Orchestra Recordings

Live sound, recording and production for the orchestra of the Escola Artística de Música do Conservatório Nacional, in Lisbon. The school plays often at the Antigo Picadeiro Real of the Museu Nacional dos Coches, in Belém, within the DGPC's "Artes no Património" programme. I worked on several of these concerts, on live sound, recording and production.

## Dolby Atmos at the Picadeiro Real

On 19 January 2024 I recorded the orchestra's concert at the Picadeiro Real live, with the microphones placed for Atmos, and mixed it in Dolby Atmos. The mix was also delivered in binaural, and the project was prepared for screening in a cinema. The video above is the binaural version: listen with headphones.

## Almada

On 24 June 2023 the same orchestra played a concert in Almada. I ran the live sound and made a multitrack recording of the concert. The Almada photos show the microphones going up on stage in the afternoon, the control position with a Yamaha digital console and a laptop recording the multitrack, and the concert in the evening.
