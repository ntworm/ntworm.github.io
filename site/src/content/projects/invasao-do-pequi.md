## Invasão do Pequi

A 2024 Palmas music video and album track, recorded locally. My role was twofold:

- **Artist** through my band <a class="entity-link" href="/work/em-agosto-chove">Em Agosto Chove</a> — the band appeared as performers on the track.
- **Mix Engineer** on the album tracks — handled the mix for the music video cut and the album session.

This sits in the long tail of the Palmas music-video work I did throughout the 2020s, alongside the Bem Ali Sessions cycle and other recordings at and around Árvore Seca. The format is the same as those: short run, tight delivery, the mix has to read well on phone speakers as well as on the festival-room playback.
